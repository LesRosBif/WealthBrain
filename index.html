Tax profile

UK · England · indicative rules for 2026/27. Tax assumptions are separate from gross wealth forecasts.

Excludes NI, savings/dividends, Scottish bands, marriage allowance, child benefit charge, pension allowance charges and other tax complexities. Not a tax return.

Tax-aware holdings

Record holdings separately from account balances. Values here are metadata only and are NOT added again to net worth.

Holdings: estimated disposal treatment

Assumes an immediate full disposal in 2026/27, no other gains, and a single annual CGT exemption. Estimates must not be added together: the annual exemption is shared across disposals. EIS/SEIS exemption requires all conditions to be satisfied, not just three years. A displayed exemption is conditional, not verified.

Realised capital gains

Track taxable disposals by owner and tax year. The £3,000 annual exemption is applied once across recorded disposals, not once per investment.

Illustrative 2026/27 rates only. Excludes carried-forward losses, residential-property rules, EIS/SEIS deferral, partial disposals and other special reliefs.

Pension tax-free cash

Standard lump sum allowance £268,275; individual protections and past crystallisations can change this. A 25% pension tax-free amount is not an unlimited entitlement.

Standard annual pension allowance £60,000 may be tapered or restricted. No lifetime allowance charge applies. Pension withdrawal tax is not yet included in forecasts.
